What Is AMUM?

AMUM stands for Affiliate Marketing Ultimate Mastery — a training built to help beginners understand affiliate marketing from the fundamentals, and learn the practical process behind promoting digital products online.

In simple terms: affiliate marketing is a model where you promote a product or service created by another business, and earn a commission when your referral leads to a sale.

  • How affiliate marketing works
  • How to choose products
  • How to identify potential customers
  • How to promote products
  • How to communicate with prospects
  • How to handle common objections
  • How to approach selling ethically
  • How to use a smartphone as a business tool
